I train deep learning models on satellite imagery. At the moment that means object detection, vision foundation models and urban-growth forecasting at [MindEarth](#runs), on data too large to fit anywhere convenient.

Before that I spent a year at the University of Bologna teaching segmentation networks to read declassified *1960s CORONA spy-satellite* photographs, looking for archaeological sites in the Mesopotamian floodplain. The model found four that nobody had mapped; field verification confirmed them.

I like problems where the pixels are old, the labels are scarce and the answer has to be explained to someone who isn't an ML person.
